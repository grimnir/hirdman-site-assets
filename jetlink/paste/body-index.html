How a security engagement runs

  1. Scope

    We agree on targets, goals and the rules of engagement.

  2. Authorization

    An NDA and written permission to test, signed before any work starts.

  3. Testing

    Our engineers work the agreed scope inside the agreed window.

  4. Report

    Every finding with evidence, severity and the steps to fix it.

  5. Retest

    Included in every test: we check your fixes and confirm what is closed.
