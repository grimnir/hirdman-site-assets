This Cookie Policy explains how Jetlink Solutions Inc ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on our websites operating under the Jetlink Solutions and Herdman brands (collectively, the "Site").

WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used by website owners to make their websites work, or to work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information.

Cookies set by the website owner (in this case, Jetlink Solutions Inc) are called "first-party cookies". Cookies set by parties other than the website owner are called "third-party cookies". Third-party cookies enable third-party features or functionality to be provided on or through the website (e.g., advertising, interactive content, and analytics). The parties that set these third-party cookies can recognize your computer both when it visits the website in question and also when it visits certain other websites.

WHY DO WE USE COOKIES?

We use first-party and third-party cookies for several reasons. Some cookies are required for technical reasons in order for our Site to operate, and we refer to these as "essential" or "strictly necessary" cookies. Other cookies enable us to track and target the interests of our users to enhance the experience on our Site. Third parties serve cookies through our Site for analytics and other purposes. This is described in more detail below.

TYPES OF COOKIES WE USE

The specific types of first-party and third-party cookies served through our Site and the purposes they perform are described below:

1. ESSENTIAL COOKIES

These cookies are strictly necessary to provide you with services available through our Site and to use some of its features.

Purpose: These cookies are essential for the Site to function properly. They enable basic functions like page navigation, access to secure areas of the website, and authentication. Without these cookies, the Site cannot function properly.

Examples:
- Session cookies that keep you logged in as you navigate through different pages
- Security cookies used to authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of login credentials
- Load balancing cookies that distribute website traffic across servers

Who serves these cookies: Jetlink Solutions Inc (first-party)

How to refuse: Because these cookies are strictly necessary to deliver the Site to you, you cannot refuse them. You can block or delete them by changing your browser settings as described below under "How can you control cookies?"

2. ANALYTICS AND PERFORMANCE COOKIES

These cookies are used to collect information about traffic to our Site and how users interact with our Site. The information gathered may include the number of visitors to the Site, the websites that referred them to the Site, the pages they visited on the Site, what time of day they visited the Site, whether they have visited the Site before, and other similar information.

Purpose: We use this information to help operate our Site more efficiently, to gather broad demographic information, monitor the level of activity on our Site, and improve the Site.

Examples:
- Google Analytics cookies that help us understand how visitors engage with our Site
- Cookies that track which pages are most and least popular
- Cookies that help us identify technical issues or errors

Who serves these cookies: Google Analytics and other analytics providers (third-party), and Jetlink Solutions Inc (first-party)

How to refuse: To refuse these cookies, please follow the instructions below under "How can you control cookies?" Alternatively, you can opt out of Google Analytics by visiting: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

3. FUNCTIONALITY COOKIES

These cookies are used to recognize you when you return to our Site. This enables us to personalize our content for you and remember your preferences (for example, your choice of language or region).

Purpose: To enhance your experience by remembering your preferences and settings.

Examples:
- Cookies that remember your language preference
- Cookies that remember your login information (if you choose "remember me")
- Cookies that remember your preferences for how the Site is displayed

Who serves these cookies: Jetlink Solutions Inc (first-party)

How to refuse: To refuse these cookies, please follow the instructions below under "How can you control cookies?" Please note that refusing these cookies may impact the functionality and user experience of our Site.

4. TARGETING AND ADVERTISING COOKIES

These cookies are used to make advertising messages more relevant to you. They perform functions like preventing the same ad from continuously reappearing, ensuring that ads are properly displayed, and in some cases selecting advertisements that are based on your interests.

Purpose: To deliver relevant advertisements and measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns.

Examples:
- Cookies that track which ads you have seen
- Cookies that limit the number of times you see an advertisement
- Cookies that help measure advertising campaign performance
- Cookies that remember websites you have visited to provide targeted advertising

Who serves these cookies: Third-party advertising networks and partners

How to refuse: To refuse these cookies, please follow the instructions below under "How can you control cookies?" You can also opt out of interest-based advertising by visiting:
- Network Advertising Initiative: http://www.networkadvertising.org/choices/
- Digital Advertising Alliance: http://www.aboutads.info/choices/
- European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EU): http://www.youronlinechoices.eu/

COOKIES USED FOR SECURITY PURPOSES

Given the nature of our business (cybersecurity, penetration testing, and data breach monitoring), we use certain cookies specifically for security purposes:

- Fraud Prevention Cookies: To detect and prevent fraudulent activities and unauthorized access attempts
- Security Verification Cookies: To verify that requests are coming from legitimate users during the verification process
- Session Management Cookies: To maintain secure sessions when you access your Data Breach Monitoring Cabinet or other secure services

These security-related cookies are essential for protecting both your data and our services from malicious actors.

HOW CAN YOU CONTROL COOKIES?

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences by clicking on the appropriate opt-out links provided in the cookie descriptions above.

Browser Controls: You can set or amend your web browser controls to accept or refuse cookies. If you choose to reject cookies, you may still use our Site, though your access to some functionality and areas of our Site may be restricted. As the means by which you can refuse cookies through your web browser controls vary from browser to browser, you should visit your browser's help menu for more information.

Cookie Preference Tool: When you first visit our Site, you will be presented with a cookie consent banner. You can manage your cookie preferences through this banner or by accessing cookie settings available on our Site.

Disabling Most Interest-Based Advertising: Most advertising networks offer you a way to opt out of interest-based advertising. If you would like to find out more information, please visit:
- http://www.aboutads.info/choices/
- http://www.youronlinechoices.com

Mobile Advertising: You can opt out of having your mobile advertising identifiers used for certain types of interest-based advertising by accessing the settings on your mobile device.

WHAT ABOUT OTHER TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES, LIKE WEB BEACONS?

Cookies are not the only way to recognize or track visitors to a website. We may use other, similar technologies from time to time, like web beacons (sometimes called "tracking pixels" or "clear gifs"). These are tiny graphics files that contain a unique identifier that enable us to recognize when someone has visited our Site or opened an email that we have sent them. This allows us, for example, to monitor the traffic patterns of users from one page within our Site to another, to deliver or communicate with cookies, to understand whether you have come to our Site from an online advertisement displayed on a third-party website, to improve site performance, and to measure the success of email marketing campaigns. In many instances, these technologies are reliant on cookies to function properly, and so declining cookies will impair their functioning.
