Who we are

Leak analysis since the 2000s

The people behind Hirdman come from the early security forums, antichat.com, cracklab, wasm.in and rdot among them, and from penetration testing. It was a large group of specialists, each strong in their own field, and for every penetration test we put together a team from the people that engagement needed. Over the years the group built up its own research, tooling and software.

We started collecting leaks early and wrote our own software to sort them. At first the leaks fed our penetration tests: we called that stage the pre-pentest. The same people have worked on investigations of real crimes in Dubai, built complex search systems and helped shut down criminal groups and drug marketplaces.

Hirdman, a Data Leak Intelligence team, is the newer brand that packages this experience into services. Behind it are hundreds of projects and penetration tests, and every team member has at least 5 years of technical experience. We keep a low profile by choice.

More than 300 companies have worked with us, in banking, fintech, government and IT. Headquarters are in Torrance, California, USA, with a presence in Canada, the UK (London), Germany, Portugal and Asia.

How we think

We look at a company like an attacker

On day one we ask the attacker's question: which systems can be reached with the data that is already out there. We build the report on that answer, with proof.

We use our own service every day

The portal our clients open is the one our analysts work in. Every record passes the same verification, the same tiers and the same hands before it reaches you.

We do the sorting

You give us a domain. Out of a large mass of varied data, your portal gets a structured list of findings in order of priority. On Enterprise our analysts also write the report for you, a finished PDF.
