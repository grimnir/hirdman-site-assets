Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Jetlink Solutions Inc. ("Hirdman", "we") collects through hirdman.pro, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. It covers this website only. Data we process for clients under contract, including breach data in a client's portal, is governed by that contract and its data processing agreement. Our Cookie Policy describes how the site uses cookies.

What we collect

What you send us
When you fill in a form or write to us: your name, work email, company and domain, and your message. We use it to answer you and to prepare the free check you asked for.
Technical data
Your browser and our web host record the IP address, browser type, pages visited and time of visit, for security and to keep the site running.
Cookies
Essential cookies keep the site working and protect it from attacks. With your consent, analytics cookies tell us which pages are read.

Why and on what basis

  • To answer your request and run the free check: steps you ask us to take before a contract.
  • To protect the site and keep it running: our legitimate interest.
  • Analytics: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Cookie settings.

We do not sell personal data.

Who processes it with us

The site runs on Tilda (hosting and forms). Form submissions reach us by email and in Tilda CRM. Fonts come from Google Fonts. Cloudflare and DDoS-Guard protect the site and measure its performance. Google Tag Manager loads analytics with your consent. Each of these providers processes data under its own terms and only for the service it gives us.

How long we keep it

Requests sent through the site: 12 months after our last contact, unless they become a contract. Technical logs: as long as our providers keep them for security.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict its use, or to stop a use based on our legitimate interest. You can withdraw consent to analytics at any time. Write to info@hirdman.pro. We answer within 30 days. If you are in the EU or the UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Security

The site uses HTTPS. Requests are read only by the people who answer them.

Changes

When this policy changes, we update the date above. We announce significant changes on this page.
