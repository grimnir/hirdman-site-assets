What stays the same at every level.

The level sets the cadence and the depth of analyst work. We do the heavy lifting at all three.

  • Collection, ETL, deduplication, verification and risk scoring run on our servers. Your portal holds the result, ready to act on.
  • Every finding reaches you with a priority and a decision: now, this week, can wait.
  • We agree the scope up front, so you know what is covered before the work starts.
  • Exports are unlimited, and you pull your data as often as you need. The price follows the depth of analyst work, with no per-asset licenses.
  • We filter out generated and repackaged sets before anything reaches you.
  • We report confirmed risks at once through the agreed channel, and the full report follows.
Brand Protection

Look-alikes, reduced to a short list of decisions.

We run the whole cycle ourselves and put in your portal only what needs action, with the grounds and the evidence for each one. You can add Brand Protection to any level. How we work on brand protection.

  1. 01

    Found

    Spelling permutations of your names, look-alike characters, other zones, your brand plus another word, and certificates issued for your names.

  2. 02

    Measured

    We measure every name that exists: mail, website, screenshot beside your own site, icon, registration, network, parking. We count the names that were never registered and set them aside.

  3. 03

    Grouped

    We find sites that wear your icon by an exact match and by shape, so we catch a recolored copy too. We work names that share an icon, a registrar or an address as one operator.

  4. 04

    Decided

    Rules and a model score each name, an analyst reviews the disputed ones and writes down the grounds. You see a verdict, an urgency in words and the evidence.

Questions before you choose.

How long does a check take?

The first picture is clear within about half an hour: how well the company is protected, what its digital hygiene is like and whether security awareness is in place. For a proof of concept, the surface report takes 1 to 2 days; the full work depends on the plan and the task.

We report a confirmed risk at once through the agreed channel; the full report with priorities, risk assessment and recommendations follows.

What does "surface assessment" mean on Control?

The full processing cycle on our side: your leaked logins, passwords and infostealer records, across every domain and subdomain, verified, deduplicated and scored, in your portal and in the report. The analyst deep dive, account validation and reverse checks start at Deep dive. Control is the entry level: a surface assessment of your exposure.

What does Deep dive cover?

Analyst work on the priority findings: high-risk account validation for up to 10 accounts, and reverse checks for up to 10 accounts with up to 5 iterations. The allowance counts the accounts analysts check. Exports and the number of domains and subdomains are unlimited.

Is Brand Protection included?

It is an add-on you can take at any level. We find look-alike names, measure them, group them by icon and owner and score them, analysts review them, and you get cases, evidence packages and the Brand report. How we work on brand protection.

What do "updated twice a month", "weekly" and "daily" mean?

How often we refresh the picture of your domains: every two weeks on Control, weekly on Deep dive, daily on Enterprise. At every level we report a confirmed risk at once, ahead of the next update. On Enterprise, we also check VIP accounts every hour.

How much does it cost?

We quote on request. The price depends on how deep the analysts go: the level and add-ons such as Brand Protection. We agree the scope up front, including compliance work: no paid modules, no per-asset licenses and no charge for talking to an analyst. Request a free check and we send a quote together with the first findings.

Are there limits on assets, users or exports?

The portal and exports are unlimited, with no per-asset or per-user licenses. What we agree up front is the analyst work for your level (for example, on Deep dive: validation for up to 10 accounts and reverse checks for up to 10 accounts with up to 5 iterations) and how many employee accounts and VIPs we monitor, so we can size the servers for you.

Can a level be changed?

Yes. We agree the scope up front and can agree it again when the task changes: start with Control for an urgent incident, move to Deep dive or Enterprise when the picture calls for analysts, daily work or Brand Protection.

What is needed to start?

For the free check, a company domain and a contact. Under contract we deploy your portal on a server you choose and load the processed data into it; the setup is ours. Analysts examine leaked credentials and exposed subdomains; we attempt access to a critical service only with your written permission.
