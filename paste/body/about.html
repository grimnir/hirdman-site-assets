Who we are

Leak analysis since the 2000s

Hirdman is a Data Leak Intelligence team. We have worked in leak analysis through hundreds of projects and penetration tests. Every team member has at least 5 years of technical experience.

Over 300 companies have worked with us, across industries including banks, fintech, government and IT. The work is the same in each: find what is exposed, verify it, rank the risk, and stay on the case until the fix is confirmed.

Headquarters are in Torrance, California, USA, with a presence in Canada, the UK (London), Germany, Portugal and Asia.

How we think

Three habits that shape every engagement.

We look at a company like an attacker

On day one we ask the attacker's question: which door opens with what is already out there. We build the report on that answer, with proof.

We use our own service every day

The portal our clients open is the one our analysts work in. Every record passes the same verification, the same tiers and the same hands before it reaches you.

We take the work on ourselves

You give us a domain. We search, verify, follow the links, rank the risk and tell you what to fix first. We deploy your portal and fill it with processed data, so the sorting stays on our side.
