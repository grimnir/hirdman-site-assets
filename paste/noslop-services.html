Breach monitoring and brand protection

The full cycle as a service. We search closed sources, verify every record, follow the links, rank the risk and tell you what to fix first. You receive the processed result in your portal, ready to act on.

What we do

  • Collection from closed forums, infostealer logs, private sources, dark web, messenger channels and breach markets.
  • Verification of every record: age, origin, and whether the set is generated or repackaged. We keep the breach date and the source date apart and state a confidence level.
  • Reverse checks from one identifier to linked accounts across services, in rounds, until the full picture is clear.
  • Attack-surface reconnaissance: domains, subdomains and exposed services, examined by analysts.
  • A risk tier for every finding, with a decision: now, this week, can wait.
  • We report confirmed risks at once through the agreed channel. On Enterprise, VIP accounts are checked every hour.

What you receive

  • A list of confirmed risks with priorities and a decision for each.
  • A report with risk assessment and step-by-step recommendations.
  • First results in 1 to 2 days.
  • Alerts via API, SIEM or email, with the full report to follow.

Brand protection

  • Lookalike domains and phishing sites, each confirmed by evidence.
  • Fake mobile apps in third-party stores, taken apart to see what they change and where the data goes.
  • Brand mentions in open and closed sources.
  • Takedown packages for registrars, app stores and lawyers: originals, hashes, timestamps and an action log.
  • An add-on at any plan level. How we work on brand protection.

Data handling

  • We process all client data on our own servers, with our own models.
  • The database is isolated from public networks.

How we handle your data

For companies that want analysts to handle leaked credentials, exposed assets and brand abuse, and their own team to receive the result ready to act on.

Penetration testing

We look at your company the way an attacker does on day one, then show which paths really lead to compromise, with proof for each.

What we do

  • External perimeter: the services reachable from the internet and how they are configured.
  • Internal network: how far an attacker gets after the first foothold.
  • Web applications and API.
  • Mobile applications.
  • Access attempts against critical services only with your written permission, inside the agreed scope.

What you receive

  • A report with real attack paths and proof of each.
  • A fix list in priority order: now, this week, can wait.
  • A plan your engineers can act on as written.

For banks, oil and gas companies, the public sector, and any company that needs to know which doors actually open.

Code analysis and reverse engineering

Software security at the level of source and at the level of machine code: your own products, software you buy, and builds you want to trust before rollout.

What we do

  • Source review: commercial scanners as the first pass, manual verification of everything they report.
  • Binary analysis at machine-code level: what the program does, hidden functions, signs of tampering.
  • Mobile app internals: how the app stores secrets and exchanges data.
  • Third-party software checked before rollout.

What you receive

  • Confirmed vulnerabilities with a reproduction path and a fix.
  • A conclusion on how the software behaves and what risks it carries.
  • A decision on whether third-party software may enter your infrastructure.

For companies that build their own software, buy it from vendors, or need to know what a ready build really does. Our engineers run reverse-engineering communities and work at machine-code level daily.

People Risk Management

Some of the most damaging incidents start inside: a current employee, a contractor, or someone who left but kept access. We help assess that risk before hiring, during employment and after departure.

What we do

  • Candidate screening within legal limits, with the candidate's written consent.
  • Insider-risk signals during employment, weighed against the person's role and access.
  • Monitoring after departure, when a former employee may still hold access or a grudge.
  • Internal incident investigations: identifying the insider behind a leak.
  • A People Risk Score for a client or counterparty, for example before a payout: how exposed their accounts are and how easily they can be taken over.

What you receive

  • A conclusion on a candidate with a risk level and the reasons behind it.
  • Signals about employees and contractors, with an explanation of why each one matters.
  • A list of accesses to revoke or review.
  • Materials for HR and legal in the volume a decision needs.

Legal frame

Every check stays within the law and your internal policies. We agree the sources, the way employees are informed and the storage of results with your legal team before the start.

  • A candidate gives written consent before screening.
  • Employees are informed in advance that corporate accounts are monitored.
  • Collection excludes private-life, health, political and religious data.

For security, HR and legal teams that need a defensible answer about the risk a person carries, within legal limits.
