No leaked password. Keys in a public file.

During attack-surface reconnaissance we found a UI development tool on two of the client's subdomains. Its version compiled the environment file into the public bundle: tokens for repositories, cloud roles, databases and storage.

The domain had no leaked emails or passwords, so a monitoring feed would have shown nothing. The vulnerability is rated low by CVSS, yet the file held live keys. One token confirmed repository access. The client was notified at once and rotation of every secret started right after.

Common questions

What a free check includes.

What do you need from us to start?

A company domain and a contact. Everything we work with has already leaked: it is in circulation on the internet and counts as compromised. We do not generate, alter or sell data, and anyone set on attacking you can take the same steps. Our edge is speed and scale: we process terabytes of data and build the links between records, which show habits, human weaknesses, password reuse and patterns. Risk is assessed on that basis.

Passwords are tested for validity and for 2FA bypass only after a contract is signed, or under a signed proof of concept if you ask for one.

How long does it take?

Initial reconnaissance takes an attacker about 30 minutes on average. Our first picture comes as fast: within half an hour of tracing the links it is clear how well the company is protected, whether it has security at all, how strong it is, what its digital hygiene is like and whether security awareness is in place. We call it the eagle-eye view.

For a proof of concept, the surface report takes 1 to 2 days. The full work depends on the plan and the task: leak assessment, insider search, targeted attacks, a pre-pentest that moves into a full penetration test, and more.

How fast do you notify us?

At once, through the official agreed channel, whichever it is: email, Telegram or another one we set up with you. Notifying you comes first. We confirm that the finding has reached you, and only then do we add it to the report. Speed of reaction is the priority, so your team can start fixing the risk immediately.

What does the report look like?

There are two kinds. The portal report is always there, in seven versions: for IT and security teams, for analysts, for the business, for compliance, a four-page executive version, the full report and one you assemble yourself. It is set to your country, with the cost of an average breach and the laws that apply, and the cost figures are updated periodically. It is generated by our algorithms, and its cost figures are estimates. It covers every record re-checked, password reuse found, statistics built, and a decision for each finding (fix now, this week, can wait) with its evidence and the fix. The same findings are available through the API.

On Enterprise, analysts also write a report for your company. It is different for every company and closes the goals you set: take it and act. It comes in two versions, one for the business and one for the IT team and analysts. It assesses your company the way an attacker would: how they would get in and what the impact would be. It then sets out how to defend: which employees need security awareness training on digital hygiene and passwords, what software to install, what to stop doing and which mistakes to avoid. The full scope of protection advice, built on your leaked data.
