API

Verified, decision-ready data.

The API returns what the analysts have confirmed: each finding with its risk tier, the decision attached to it and the evidence behind it. The same picture you see in the portal, in a form your systems can read.

  • Findings with a tier and a decision: now, this week, can wait.
  • Linked accounts from reverse checks, so one record is reported with its context.
  • Status of each finding as it moves from reported to fixed and re-checked.
  • We set up access during onboarding, within the scope agreed for your engagement.

Before you write to us.

Can we show Hirdman findings in our own product?

Yes. Partners pull verified findings through the API and present them in their platform, or deliver white-label reports to their clients.

Do alerts contain raw dumps?

Alerts carry verified findings with their evidence and risk tier. Raw material stays in Hirdman infrastructure, and we show it within the agreed scope.

Where does the data stay?

We process all client data on our own servers with local models, and your portal runs on the server you choose.

How do we start a partnership?

Write to info@hirdman.pro with a short description of your service and clients. We reply to every request.
