How the check runs

Three things to know before you send.

No installation

You give us a domain. We search the sources, verify every record, rank the risk and tell you what to fix first. Nothing to deploy or configure on your side.

Only with your permission

Analysts examine leaked credentials and exposed subdomains. Access to a critical service is attempted only with your written permission. Otherwise the check stays on the data.

Processing stays on our servers

Own servers and local models. Client data never passes through public AI services or third-party clouds, and the database is not reachable from public networks.
