What happens next

  1. RequestYou send a company domain and a contact. That is all we need to begin.
  2. Discussing the task and formatA short exchange on what you want to know, which level fits, and how you want to receive results: portal, report, API, SIEM or email.
  3. AgreementWe agree the scope up front, with no per-asset licenses or export limits.
  4. Analysis and reportThe first picture within about half an hour, a surface report in 1 to 2 days. We report confirmed risks at once through the agreed channel; the full report follows with priorities, risk assessment and recommendations.
